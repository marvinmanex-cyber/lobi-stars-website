<p>Lobi Stars Football Club will be aiming to bounce back in emphatic fashion when they host Group D table-topping Yobe Desert Stars FC in a Matchday Three fixture of the 2026/2027 Nigeria National League (NNL) season at McCarthy Stadium, Makurdi, on Sunday, November 22, at 4:00pm.</p><p>The Alia Boys, fresh from a narrow and controversial  away defeat to Kada Warriors in Zaria, where a home player received two yellow cards without expulsion , have quickly turned the page, with Coach Bala Nikyu and his charges shifting focus to a crucial home assignment against the high-flying Damaturu-based Club. </p><p>Having resumed training with almost all the squad members available, the Pride of Benue are poised for a determined response, as they seek to deliver a performance worthy of their supporters' expectation. The technical crew will be counting on discipline, tactical cohesion and clinical match execution to secure maximum points and restore smiles to the faces of the Shoe Get Size faithful.</p><p>Meanwhile, the club’s management, under Simon Terver Ikya, continues to intensify efforts towards creating the necessary conditions for the team’s success, with the protection and advancement of elite football remaining a central priority.</p><p>Yobe Desert Stars, however, arrive in Makurdi with formidable credentials. The Conference D leaders have enjoyed a blistering start to the campaign, winning  the first two fixtures, scoring five goals and conceding only two to amass six points. With a balanced squad, attacking verve and a tenacious never-say-die mentality, the visitors are expected to pose a stern challenge.</p><p>But Lobi Stars, with a rich pedigree, experience and stature in Nigerian football, will be determined not to squander the opportunity to reclaim momentum on home soil. For the Shoe Get Size, Sunday presents more than just three points; it is an opportunity to make a statement of intent, as they remain focused on returning to the elite league.</p><p>The encounter will be streamed live by Spotlight Hub, the NNL’s official live-streaming partner for the 2026/2027 season, giving supporters beyond the confines of McCarthy Stadium an opportunity to witness the encounter.</p><p>With anticipation building and the stakes steadily rising, Makurdi is set for an intriguing football spectacle as Lobi Stars seek to turn their home turf into a fortress once again.</p>
