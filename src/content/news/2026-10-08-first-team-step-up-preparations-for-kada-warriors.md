The Lobi Stars FC First Team were back on the grass early on Thursday as preparations for the visit of Kada Warriors FC moved up a gear.

The session focused on quick passing in tight spaces, pressing triggers and set-pieces, with the technical staff keen to build on the intensity shown in recent weeks.

## Fitness boost

The medical department reported a clean bill of health for the bulk of the squad, with every player who took part completing the full session.

> "The energy in the group is very good. Everyone knows what this fixture means to the supporters in Makurdi." – a member of the coaching staff

## What's next

The squad will hold two further sessions before the matchday walk-through at McCarthy Stadium. Tickets for the game are on sale now on the [tickets page](/tickets).
