Buying a Lobi Stars FC ticket online takes just a few minutes.

1. Visit the [tickets page](/tickets) and choose your match.
2. Pick your seating area and the number of tickets.
3. Enter your name and email address.
4. Pay securely with Paystack.

Your tickets arrive by email with a unique QR code for each seat. If you can't find the email, check your spam or promotions folder.
