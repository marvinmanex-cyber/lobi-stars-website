Tickets for Lobi Stars FC vs Kada Warriors FC on Friday 23 October 2026 are now on sale.

## How to buy

1. Go to the [tickets page](/tickets).
2. Choose the match and your seating area: Regular, Premium or VIP.
3. Pay securely online with Paystack.
4. Your ticket, with its QR code, is emailed to you straight away.

## On matchday

Show the QR code on your phone at the gate, where it will be scanned by stewards. Each QR code can only be used once, so please don't share it.
