Lobi Stars FC is taking football into the heart of the community with a series of free football clinics across Makurdi.

Over the coming weeks, club staff and First Team players will visit schools and local pitches to run fun, open sessions for everyone who loves the game.

## Why it matters

The club has been part of life in Benue State since 1969. The clinics are one way of giving back to the supporters and communities who have stood by the Red and Gold.

Dates and venues will be announced on the club's official channels.
