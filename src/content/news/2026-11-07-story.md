<p>Lobi Stars Football Club will commence their 2026/2027 Nigeria National League (NNL) campaign on Sunday, November 8, 2026 by 4PM, with an enthralling encounter against Bichi First FC at the McCarthy Stadium, Makurdi, as the Pride of Benue seek an emphatic opening-day victory in their quest for promotion to the Nigeria Premier Football League (NPFL).</p><p></p><p>Having devoted considerable time to pre-season preparations, the Alia Boys approach the fixture with renewed tactical impetus and a clear appreciation of the demands of the campaign ahead. A positive result against the Kano-based newcomers would provide an important competitive foundation and the psychological impetus required for the arduous journey towards top-flight football.</p><p>Head Coach Bala Nikyu has overseen an intensive preparatory programme, complemented by a series of friendly fixtures which have offered the technical crew an opportunity to assess tactical combinations, evaluate personnel and consolidate the team's competitive structure. The recruitment and unveiling of more than a dozen new players have further strengthened the squad, introducing fresh legs, additional options and renewed competition for places across the various departments.</p><p>On the opposing bench, Bichi First FC will be under the guidance of experienced tactician Patrick Bassey, whose appointment is expected to bring greater tactical organisation, discipline and a strong developmental orientation to the Kano-based outfit. Founded in 2025 by Hon. Abubakar Kabir Bichi, the club acquired the NNL slot of Kano Ambassador FC and has since embarked on an ambitious project centred on youth development and the advancement of football in the region. Their preparations for the new season, including pre-season engagements, suggest a side eager to establish its credentials at this level.</p><p>Sunday's encounter will mark the first meeting between both clubs, introducing an unfamiliar opponent into Lobi Stars' immediate campaign equation. However, the Pride of Benue are under no illusion about the quality of opposition they will face and are prepared to impose their authority through tactical discipline, purposeful ball progression and clinical execution in the final third.</p><p>Off the pitch, Club Chairman, Simon Terver Ikya, has continued to provide the necessary administrative and logistical support to ensure that the players and technical crew have a conducive environment to prosecute the club's objectives for the season. Ikya is leaving no stone unturned as he has boasted the home support base by throwing open the popular side gate for free access to the 12th player.</p><p>With the campaign's first three points at stake, the message to the Makurdi faithful is unequivocal: this is football on home soil. It is not Jos, Bauchi, Enugu or Lafia; it is Makurdi, and the McCarthy Stadium is the place to be.</p><p>Lobi Stars call on their supporters, football enthusiasts and the good people of Benue State to turn out in their numbers, rally behind the team and make their voices count as the Pride of Benue begin their promotion pursuit. Bichi First FC may arrive with ambitions of their own, but Lobi Stars will not yield their home advantage without a fight.<br></p>
