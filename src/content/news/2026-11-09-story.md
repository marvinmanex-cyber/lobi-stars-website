<p>𝗠𝗔𝗧𝗖𝗛 𝗥𝗘𝗣𝗢𝗥𝗧 </p><p></p><p>𝗙𝗢𝗚𝗔 𝗙𝗜𝗥𝗘𝗦 𝗟𝗢𝗕𝗜 𝗦𝗧𝗔𝗥𝗦 𝗧𝗢 𝗪𝗜𝗡𝗡𝗜𝗡𝗚 𝗦𝗧𝗔𝗥𝗧 𝗜𝗡 𝗡𝗡𝗟</p><p>Lobi Stars Football Club on Sunday, November 8, 2026 commenced their 2026/2027 Nigeria National League (NNL) campaign on a winning note, securing a deserved 1-0 victory over newly promoted Bichi First FC of Kano at the McCarthy Stadium, Makurdi.</p><p>The Pride of Benue seized the initiative from the opening exchanges, dictating the tempo, dominating possession and mounting sustained attacking pressure. Their early dominance yielded dividends when Ibrahim Yahaya produced an impressive individual effort, dribbling past opposing defenders before delivering a fine cross into the penalty area for Dominic Foga, who calmly tucked the ball beyond the goalkeeper to hand Lobi Stars the lead.</p><p>Despite maintaining their territorial advantage, the Alia Boys could not extend their lead before the interval, as the first half ended 1-0.</p><p>Bichi First FC emerged with greater attacking intent after the restart, seeking an equaliser. However, Lobi Stars' defensive organisation proved formidable, with goalkeeper Daniel Atsaka and the central defensive pairing marshalled by Francis Odinaka frustrating the visitors' advances. </p><p>Odinaka's commanding display subsequently earned him the Man of the Match award.</p><p>Despite late attempts by the visitors to salvage a point, Lobi Stars remained resolute to preserve their slender advantage and secure all three points in their season opener.</p><p>Speaking to sports journalists after the encounter, Lobi Stars Head Coach, Bala Nikyu, commended his players for their resilience, acknowledging the challenges that often accompany opening fixtures. He expressed satisfaction with the result while admitting that certain areas required improvement, assuring that necessary adjustments would be made ahead of subsequent matches.</p><p>On his part, Bichi First FC Head Coach, Patrick Bassey, acknowledged the steady improvement of the Nigeria National League, expressed satisfaction with his players' performance and commended both teams for an entertaining contest.</p><p>Meanwhile, Lobi Stars Chairman, Simon Terver Ikya, described the three points as the most important outcome, reaffirming the club's determination to sustain its promotion ambition.</p><p>Ikya also appreciated the supporters for their impressive turnout and vocal backing, urging them to maintain their support as the team pursues its season objectives.</p><p>Lobi Stars will now shift their focus to their first away fixture of the season against Kada Warriors of Kaduna, as they seek to build on their impressive start to the campaign.</p>
