McCarthy Stadium is home to Lobi Stars FC. Here's how to make the most of your matchday.

## Before you travel

- Buy your ticket online in advance from the [tickets page](/tickets).
- Keep your QR code ready on your phone.

## At the stadium

- Gates open two hours before kick-off.
- Follow stewards' directions to your seating area.
- Food and drinks can be ordered from the club's matchday food partner.

## Getting home

Please leave the stadium calmly and follow the directions of stewards and security staff.
