<p>Ropajo Hotels &amp; Resorts Kits Lobi Stars With One-Year Training Jersey Deal</p><p></p><p>Ropajo Hotels &amp; Resorts has entered into a one-year partnership with Lobi Stars Football Club of Makurdi for the provision of training jerseys for the team.</p><p>The deal was unveiled in Makurdi by the Chairman of Lobi Stars, Terver Ikya, alongside management of Ropajo Hotels &amp; Resorts.</p><p>Under the agreement, the Pride of Benue will wear the orange training jerseys boldly branded with Ropajo on the front throughout the one-year period.</p><p>The jerseys, as displayed during the unveiling ceremony, feature the Ropajo Hotels &amp; Resorts name across the chest, with the Lobi Stars crest on the left side of the chest.</p><p>Speaking at the unveiling, Chairman Terver Ikya described the partnership as a timely boost for the club and a demonstration of growing confidence by the private sector in Benue football.</p><p>Lobi Stars, who are currently rebuilding with an eye on a return to the Nigeria Premier Football League (NPFL), will benefit from the provision of standard training kits for players and officials, a key requirement for modern football preparation.</p><p>For Ropajo Hotels &amp; Resorts, one of Makurdi's leading hospitality brands, the partnership offers massive brand visibility. The Ropajo logo will feature prominently in training sessions, team photographs, videos and other media content of the club throughout the duration of the deal.</p><p>Stakeholders say the agreement is another example of local businesses stepping in to support local sports, a model that is vital for the sustainability of football clubs in Nigeria.</p><p>Both parties expressed optimism that the partnership will go beyond the one-year training jersey deal and grow into a broader and long-term relationship for the development of football in Benue State.</p><p>Signed </p><p>Uja Emmanuel <br>Media Director <br>Lobi Stars FC<br>Monday , Nov 9,2026</p><p>-</p>
