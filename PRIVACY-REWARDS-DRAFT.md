# Privacy Policy: draft additions for Lobi Stars Rewards

**Status: DRAFT, not on the website.** For the club and its legal adviser / Data
Protection Officer to review. Once approved, the web team adds it to
lobistarsfc.com/privacy (`src/pages/privacy.astro`) and updates the date.

Everything below describes what the website actually does today. Items in
**[square brackets]** are decisions for the club.

## 2. New subsection after "Our fan database": "Lobi Stars Rewards"

- **Joining is your choice.** Rewards is free and optional. You can use the
  website, buy tickets and play Predict & Win without joining.
- **Your member card** on My Rewards shows a QR code. It contains only your
  member number and a security code, **no name, email or phone**. Club staff
  scan it at the stadium food stands and club shop to see the vouchers you can
  use.
- **Tiers and badges** are worked out from your points history. They are not
  shared outside the club.
- **Fraud checks.** To keep the programme fair, club staff see a list of
  accounts worth checking (for example several accounts created on one device,
  many points earned in a week, or points taken back after refunds). A person
  always checks before any action. If your membership is put on hold, you can
  contact us to explain.
- **Reports.** Staff see totals (members, points issued and used, members per
  tier). Downloads that include personal data are restricted to authorised
  staff and every download is logged. Sponsors and partners **never** receive
  your personal data, only anonymous totals.
- **Messages.** We show Rewards messages on My Rewards. We email you only the
  kinds you chose under "Email me about", except emails you ask for directly
  (such as your voucher code or a sign-in code).
- **Leaving.** You can leave Rewards or ask us to delete your Rewards data at
  any time (see "Your rights"). Your Star Points end when you leave; they have
  no cash value.
