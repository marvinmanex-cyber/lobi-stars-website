# Lobi Stars FC: Matchday Live staff guide

This guide covers everything staff do for a **Lobi Stars home game** on the
website: the stream, the squad, Kick-off and Full-time, Man of the Match
voting and Predict & Win. Away games only need the score (they can still have
live commentary on Lobi Stars FC Live).

Sign in at **https://lobistarsfc.com/admin/login** with your own staff email
and password (the owner can use the admin code). All times on the website use
the club's server clock in **Nigerian time**, never your phone's clock.

---

## What fans get on a home game

Every home game has a **Matchday Live** page (from Fixtures, the homepage or
the Match Centre) with four tabs:

| Tab | What it does | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Watch Live** | Plays the YouTube or Facebook stream; afterwards it becomes the "Full match replay" | Stream starts at kick-off |
| **Commentary** | Plays Lobi Stars FC Live commentary right on the page (same player as lobistarsfc.com/commentary) | From the commentary start time (normally 15 min before kick-off) until 15 min after full time |
| **Vote MOTM** | Fans vote for a Lobi Stars player on the field | Automatic, fixed times: opens at kick-off (4:00pm), closes 1 hour 50 minutes later (**5:50pm**) |
| **Predict & Win** | Fans predict the exact score; earliest correct prediction wins ₦10,000 | 24 hours before kick-off until kick-off |

Fans need a free fan account with a confirmed email to vote or predict.

Fans who turned on **🔔 Match alerts** also get phone notifications: when the
radio show goes on air, at kick-off, for every goal, at half-time and at full
time. These go out **by themselves** from the buttons below; see
STAFF-GUIDE section 26 (Admin → Matchday → Fan Match Alerts).

## 1. Before the match (a day or more ahead)

**Start with the Match Checklist** (**Admin → Matchday → Match Checklist**):
it lists everything to do before, during and after this match, with who owns
each task and when it is due, and a **Go to task →** button to the right
page. Set a task to *Submitted for review* when it's done; a Super Admin
ticks it off. Print it (**Print view**) for a clipboard on matchday. A daily
reminder email starts 3 days before kick-off. Full details: STAFF-GUIDE.md,
section 35.

1. **Check the match** in **Admin → League → Fixtures** (the whole season is
   loaded): date, kick-off time and venue. Ticket prices are set in
   **Admin → Matchday → Match Tickets & Settings**.
2. Open **Admin → Matchday → Live Match Control** (the Match Centre) and choose the match.
3. **Matchday squad:** in *Matchday squad*, set each player to **Starting XI**,
   **Bench** or *Not selected*, and press **Save Match Centre**. **Or** pick the
   line-up in the **Media Centre** (Matchday Desk → Line-up): it counts for
   voting from kick-off even before it is approved, and it fills an empty
   squad here. Nothing is shown to fans before kick-off.
   - **Who fans can vote for:** the Starting XI, and a substitute only once a
     **Substitution** for them is added (Live timeline here, or a Media Centre
     live update): put the player **coming on** as the player, and "on for X"
     in the detail. Unused substitutes can't be voted for.
   - Players come from the Squad (the Content CMS, or profiles verified in
     Matchday › Squad: Profile Submissions). Add new players there first.
4. **Stream links:** in *Live stream (Watch Live)*, paste the **YouTube live
   link** and/or the **Facebook live link** for this game and press **Save**.
   Leave them empty to show links to the official channels instead.
   - Only YouTube and Facebook links are accepted.
5. **Live commentary:** in **Admin → Match Tickets & Settings**, edit the match and check
   *Live commentary available for this match* is ticked. Change *Commentary
   starts* only if it shouldn't start 15 minutes before kick-off. This works
   for home **and** away games. On the day, press **Test stream** on
   **Admin → Commentary** (see STAFF-GUIDE.md, section 9).
6. **In-seat food:** check the menu (**Food: Menu & Prices**): prices, photos,
   which dishes are ON (ready) or OFF, and that ordering is **Automatic** or will be opened.
   Set up the kitchen tablet with **Food: Live Order Board** and make sure the
   kitchen and delivery staff have notifications on (STAFF-GUIDE.md, sections 13–16).
6. **Tickets:** to give members first access, set *General sale opens* on the
   match (Admin → Match Tickets & Settings). Members confirmed in Admin → Members can
   buy before then; everyone else from that time.
7. Predict & Win opens automatically **24 hours before kick-off**. Fans who
   said yes to news and offers get a "Predictions are now open" email (once
   the email service is set up).

> You can update the squad and stream links right up to kick-off.

## 1c. MatchDay Kitchen on the day (9 November 2026)

- **The day before:** a kitchen supervisor opens MatchDay Kitchen › **Sales reports** › **How much to prepare** and buys stock from it (the last 5 matches plus 15%).
- **Two hours before kick-off:** turn on the **collection board** on the TV at the pickup point: **lobistarsfc.com/kitchen/board/**, then tap the screen once.
- **Menu and prices:** check **Goal deals on** (and the percent), **Half-time slots** (orders per slot) and **Pay by bank transfer** are set the way you want.
- **During the match:** a Lobi Stars goal entered in Live Match Control starts the goal deal by itself. Expect a rush of orders in the next few minutes.
- **Half-time:** orders are due at 45, 50 and 55 minutes after kick-off, in slots. Cook in that order.

Full details: STAFF-GUIDE.md › section 42 › Kitchen extras.

## 1a. The digital match day programme (2 days before)

In **Admin → Matchday → Matchday Programme**, press **+ New programme**, pick the
fixture, upload the 4-page PDF, add quick links and choose **Schedule → 24 hours
before kick-off** (or Publish now). Make the adverts in **QR advert campaigns**: an A4 poster for the gates
(campaign gate), the big-screen slide (screen) and a WhatsApp status (status).
Then press **Share** on the programme: post to Telegram (automatic), copy the
captions for Facebook, X, WhatsApp, Instagram and TikTok, or schedule the
Telegram post for when the programme goes live. Full steps: STAFF-GUIDE section 28.

## 1b. About 30 minutes before kick-off (optional)

Want to remind fans the pre-match show is about to start? Go to
**Admin → Matchday → Fan Match Alerts**, tap **🎙️ Pre-match show starting**,
check the text and press **Send alert**. (When Lobi Stars FC Live goes on air,
fans who chose radio get "Pre-match show is live" automatically, so you
don't need to send both.)

**Gate and kitchen helpers (the day before or on the morning):** the Matchday
Operations Manager opens **Admin › Commerce › Matchday Helpers** and makes a
login for each helper: name, phone, **Gate** or **Kitchen**, and the match.
Each gets a username and a 6-digit PIN, plus a QR code / link. Press **Share on
WhatsApp** to send it to their phone. The helper opens the link (or goes to
**lobistarsfc.com/helper** and types the username and PIN) and lands straight on
their screen: the ticket scanner or the Kitchen order board. They stay signed
in all day on that phone, and their login stops at **23:59** on match day.
Lost PIN: **New PIN**. Someone leaves early: **Switch off**. Running late:
**Later finish** (until 06:00 next morning at most). Each scan and kitchen step
shows the helper's name.

**At the gates: Lobi Gate Control (lobistarsfc.com/lobi-gate-control).** Each
scanner phone is switched on once with its passcode from the admin. A Ticket
Steward works at one gate (set by the club in Admin › Commerce › Lobi Gate
Control, or chosen on the phone); a Gate Supervisor has all gates. They
press **Scan ticket** to use the phone camera. If a ticket won't scan, they type
the 12 letters and numbers after LS-TIX-. Each answer shows the buyer's name,
stand, adult or child, price paid, when and where it was bought, and whether it
is genuine: green ADMIT, red ALREADY USED (with when, which gate and who), red
FAKE TICKET, amber WRONG MATCH, red CANCELLED or NOT PAID. Gate Supervisors can
re-admit a fan who stepped out (with a reason). **Today** shows check-ins per
stand and gate, live. Full guide: STAFF-GUIDE §43.

**Match-day gate checklist (Admin › Commerce › Lobi Gate Control):**
1. The day before: stand sizes set; banned list up to date; staff and media
   passes printed (**Staff & media passes**); report email addresses right.
2. Two hours before the gates open: every scanner shows **✓ Ready** (fix what a
   ✗ lists); stewards have practised (**Practice mode**).
3. The gates open automatically (default 3 hours before kick-off), or press
   **Open the gates now**.
4. During the match: watch **How full each stand is**, **Queue speed** and the
   watch list; message a gate or one scanner when needed.
5. After the last fans are in: **Close the gates**. The match-day report is
   emailed automatically. Full guide: STAFF-GUIDE §43a and §43b.

**Older scanner:** the Ticket Scanner (lobistarsfc.com/scan) also reads match
tickets and season tickets alike. A season ticket gets in once per home match:
if it shows "Already used for this match", the code has already been let in
today. Season tickets only work on home match days.

A match ticket only gets in on the day of **its own match**. If the scanner
shows "Wrong match: this ticket is for … on …", the fan has brought a ticket
for a different fixture: don't let them in on it. That ticket is not used up
and still works on its own match day. Send the fan to the ticket desk if they
need a ticket for today.

The scanner screen has three answers for match tickets (the scanner is also
at Admin › Matchday › Ticket Scanner, lobistarsfc.com/admin/tickets/scan):

| Screen | Means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| **Green: VALID: ADMIT** | Right match, not used yet | Let the fan in. The ticket is now used. |
| **Red: ALREADY USED** | Scanned before; the time is shown ("scanned 9 Nov, 15:41") | Don't let them in. Call the supervisor if the fan says it's their first time. |
| **Amber: WRONG MATCH** | A ticket for another match | Don't let them in on it; it still works on its own match day. |
| **Red: DO NOT ADMIT** | Not a real ticket, or cancelled (refunded) | Don't let them in. Send them to the ticket desk. |

A fan who bought several matches in one payment has one QR code per match per
person: they must show the code for **today's** match.

**Sharing the match page:** in Admin, Matchday, Share any page, type the opponent's name, press Check, then use Post now (Telegram, Facebook) or Open post window and Mark as posted. Fans can also press Share on the match page.

**Postponed?** In **League → Fixtures** press **Postpone** on the match and
give the reason; fans see "Postponed" with the reason, and the match page,
tickets and alerts follow. Reschedule it there when the new date is known.

## 2. At kick-off

In **Admin → Matchday → Live Match Control** (the Match Centre), choose the match and press
**▶ Start Match (Kick-off)**.

- The match shows as **LIVE** everywhere, with a red "LIVE NOW" banner on the homepage.
- **Man of the Match voting** is automatic at fixed times: it opens at the
  scheduled kick-off and closes 1 hour 50 minutes later (4:00pm to 5:50pm for
  a 4pm kick-off). Nothing you press changes these times.
- **Predict & Win closes** (it also closes by itself at the scheduled kick-off time).
- Opted-in fans get a "Kick-off! Vote for Man of the Match" email.
- Fans with match alerts get a **⚽ Kick-off!** phone notification.

During the match you can add goals, cards and substitutions in the *Live
timeline* and update the score, then press **Save Match Centre**. Fans see
updates within 30 seconds. **Each goal you save sends a goal alert** to fans'
phones, and setting the status to half-time (and back to live) sends the
half-time and second-half alerts, so check the score before saving.

---

**Receipts:** after delivery, press **WhatsApp** (or **Email**) under
"Receipt to the buyer" on the Delivered card to send the buyer their
MatchDay Kitchen receipt.

**During the match (in-seat food):** keep the Live Order Board open; prepare
only **Paid** orders; turn a dish **OFF** as soon as it isn't ready or runs out (MatchDay Kitchen › **Menu and prices**), and **ON** when it's ready. Before the gates open, check **Turn on order alerts** is done on every kitchen phone and tablet (press **Send a test alert**) and tap each screen once so the sound works. A supervisor starts the match-day shift on **My shift**, and everyone presses **Start shift**, or they won't hear alerts. Make sure a supervisor or Super Admin is on duty: only they can confirm or reject orders, and orders keep beeping until they do. **🍳 Start cooking** beeps until someone presses Start cooking. When a fan sends a WhatsApp payment receipt, press **Record payment** on the order, add the receipt and press **Mark order paid** (STAFF-GUIDE §42).

## 3. At the final whistle

Press **■ End Match (Full-time)** and enter the **final score**, then
**Confirm full-time**.

- Man of the Match voting isn't affected: it closes at its fixed time and
  fans see the result 5 minutes later.
- The **Predict & Win winner** is worked out straight away and the message
  tells you if there is a winner.
- **Forgot to press End Match?** The match closes by itself **2 hours 15
  minutes after kick-off**. Then press **End Match** to enter the final score
  (this is what decides the Predict & Win winner).
- **Score entered wrongly?** Correct the score in *Status & score* and press
  **Save Match Centre**. The Predict & Win winner is recalculated.
- **Player stats** (the same evening): in *Player stats* press **Fill from
  matchday squad**, then enter each player's **minutes**, goals, assists and
  cards and press **Save Match Centre**. They feed /stats and the season tab on
  every player's profile page (staff guide, "Entering player stats").

## 3a. The other conference games (Website & Data Officer)

On every matchday, keep **Matchday › Other Games** open on your phone.
- At each kick-off, half-time and full time, set the score with **−** / **+**,
  press the status and **Save my report**.
- A result only counts when two independent sources agree (🟢). One report
  alone (🟡) waits for a second source; 🔴 means sources disagree: check with
  the clubs and use **Set verified result**.
- Club officers send their scores through their private link; you see them
  appear under **Reports received**.
- When the NNL publishes a result, use **Mark as official result** with a note
  of where it came from.
- Scores sent to you on WhatsApp: paste them in **Matchday › Score Messages**.
  Check **Waiting for you** there whenever the bell shows "score reports need
  checking".

- **Our own match counts too:** Live Match Control is one source. At full
  time, also enter the final score in Other Games (that is the second
  source), or press **Set verified result**. Otherwise our result won't be in
  the table.
- A red card ("Sources disagree") means: compare the scores and press
  **Accept this score** on the right one, or **Enter correct score**.
- About two hours after kick-off you get a reminder for any match without a
  confirmed result.

Full instructions: STAFF-GUIDE.md section 37.

## 3b. Scores Update Centre: live scores and the table (9 November 2026)

**lobistarsfc.com/scores-centre** is the page for updating NNL Conference D scores during games, on a phone, tablet or laptop. It isn't linked from the public website.

**Signing in**
- Type **your name** and the **access code** (the club gives it to the people who update scores; never post it publicly).
- You stay signed in until **2 hours pass without using the page**. Then sign in again.
- 5 wrong codes from one connection block sign-in from there for 15 minutes.
- Every score you update is recorded with your name (League History and the Activity Log).

**Updating scores**
1. Choose the week at the top (**Week 1**, **Week 2** …). It opens on the week being played, or the next one.
2. On the match, press **+** or **−** under each team's score. Pressing **+** on a match that hasn't started sets it to **Live**.
3. Tap the status: **Not started**, **Live**, **Half-time**, **Full time**, **Postponed**, **Abandoned** or **Cancelled**.
4. Press **Update**. The card turns red while it has changes that aren't saved yet.
5. The league table on the page (beside the matches, or underneath on a phone) changes straight away, with ▲▼ arrows for places gained or lost.

Update as the game goes: each goal, half-time, then **Full time** at the end. Every update replaces the one before.

**Entering old results:** pressing **+** on a match that kicked off more than 2½ hours ago sets it to **Full time** (not Live). Saving a match as Live or Half-time more than 3 hours after kick-off asks whether it should be Full time. A match left on **Live** only counts in the public table when "Count live matches in the table" is ticked.

**Checks before saving:** the page asks first when you take a goal away, set **Full time** ("Confirm full time: …"), change a match that is already finished, or update a match someone else is covering.

**Two people on the same match:** if someone else changed the match after you opened it, the page says who and what it is now, and asks before replacing it with yours.

**No network:** if the signal drops when you press Update, the update is kept on your phone ("⏳ Waiting for network") and sent by itself when the signal returns. A yellow bar at the top says how many are waiting. Don't sign out while updates are waiting. If someone changed the match in the meantime, yours isn't sent and you're told.

**Live now:** the red **Live now** button (first in the week list) shows today's matches, live ones first. The page opens there on match days.

**The clock:** live matches show the minute (12', 45+2', HT, 67', FT), counted from the planned kick-off. Under **Goals, cards, more**:
- **Kicked off now** if the match started late.
- **Second half started** when the second half begins (it also sets the match to Live). Changing Half-time to Live does the same.

**Goals, cards, more** (on each match):
- **Goals and red cards:** choose Goal, Penalty goal, Own goal or Red card, the team, the player (optional) and the minute, then **Add**. For an own goal, choose the team that gained the goal. They show on the match and, once the score counts, under that match on the public table's scoreboard. ✕ removes one (it stays in the history).
- **Match note:** e.g. "Kick-off delayed 30 min" or "Abandoned at 70', floodlights". Shown on the match.
- **I'm covering this match:** others see "Covered by …" and are asked before updating it.
- **History:** every change (who, score, time), with **Undo last change** (back to the score before) and **Remove all scores set here**.

**Who's online:** under the week title ("Online now: …").

**On the phone:** the screen stays on while the page is open (☀ Screen on). Press **Install app** in the top bar (Android, laptop), or on iPhone Share › **Add to Home Screen**, to open it like an app.

**Super Admins:** Admin › Matchday › **Scores Update Centre** shows who is signed in (and who is online now), signs one person or everyone out, changes the access code, and lists wrong-code attempts.

**Good to know**
- **Undo changes** forgets what you changed on a card before you press Update.
- **Clear** removes the score set here for a match (for example on the wrong match). The match then follows its other score sources, such as Live Match Control.
- The page checks for other people's updates every 20 seconds (never while you have changes that aren't saved).
- **Recent updates** shows the last updates and who made them.
- A score from here is a **staff-verified score**: it counts in the table at once and replaces scores from other sources for that match, including Live Match Control. For Lobi Stars' own match, keep Live Match Control and this page the same, or update only here.
- **The public table** (lobistarsfc.com/table, the homepage and Matchday Live) counts live matches as they stand only when **Count live matches in the table** is ticked (Admin › League › Table & Rules). Otherwise it changes at full time. The table in the Scores Update Centre always counts live matches.
- To change the access code: a Super Admin uses Admin › Matchday › Scores Update Centre › **Access code** (tick "Also sign everyone out" to make everyone sign in again with the new code).

## 4. Check the Predict & Win winner and pay the prize

Open **Admin → Predict & Win** and choose the match.

1. The top box shows the **final score**, the **winner's name, phone and
   email**, their prediction and the exact time it was received.
2. **Call the winner** on that phone number (they have also been emailed) and
   arrange for them to collect the ₦10,000 at the club. You may check their ID
   matches their fan account.
3. When the prize has been handed over, tick **Prize paid**. Your name and
   the date are recorded.
4. **No exact prediction?** The page says nobody has won. The prize is not
   given out and does not roll over.

**Never ask a winner for bank details, passwords or PINs through the website.**

### Disqualifying an entry
In the *Entries* list, press **Disqualify** next to an entry and give a reason
(e.g. "duplicate account"). The winner is recalculated automatically: the next
earliest correct entry wins. **Reinstate** undoes it.

### Club staff and players can't win
If an entrant is club staff, a player or a family member, press **Mark club
staff/player** next to their entry. That account can never win; the winner
is recalculated.

## 4a. Final-score reminder and Predict & Win results

- **Predict & Win** opens 24 hours before kick-off and closes at kick-off. Each fan account (one email
  address) can predict once.
- The result only shows to fans **after you enter the final score** (End Match). Fans then see the
  winner and **everyone who predicted the exact score, in order, with the exact time** (to the
  thousandth of a second). The first one wins; club staff/players and disqualified entries can't win.
  Names show as first name, surname initial and a hidden phone number.
- **Forgot the score?** From **1 hour 55 minutes after kick-off (5:55pm for a 4pm game)**, every admin
  page a Super Admin has open shows a **red, blinking alert that beeps every 4 seconds** until the final
  score is entered or a Super Admin presses **Dismiss** (recorded in the Activity Log). Tap the page
  once if the beep can't be heard: browsers only play sound after a tap.

## 4b. Man of the Match: how it works now (8 November 2026)

- **Automatic:** voting opens at kick-off (4:00pm) and closes 110 minutes later (5:50pm). At 5:50pm the
  winner is counted and announced on https://lobistarsfc.com/man-of-the-match/ by itself, with the top 3
  players' votes and percentages. Nobody approves it.
- **Who can be voted for:** the starting XI (from Live Match Control's squad, or the Media Centre line-up),
  and a substitute from the moment his **Substitution** is saved in the Live timeline (or sent as a Media
  Centre live update). The website records the time each timeline entry is saved.
- **Counted votes:** one per verified fan account. Accounts can be created any time before kick-off;
  votes from accounts made after kick-off are kept but not counted ("held"). Level on votes: whoever reached that total first wins.
- **Sponsor:** Admin → Matchday → **Man of the Match Sponsor** (Super Admins): photo, display name,
  sponsor line and a Show sponsor switch. Every change is in the Activity Log.

## 5. Man of the Match results

In **Admin → Matchday → Live Match Control** (the Match Centre), home games have a **Man of the Match votes**
panel. It says when voting opens and closes. Staff can see the live vote count at any time, so
the winner can be read out in the stadium as soon as voting closes (5:50pm for a 4pm kick-off).
Fans only see the total number of voters until 5 minutes after voting closes. Ties give joint winners. Each
player's number of awards appears on their profile automatically.

## 5a. Season Prediction League

Every Predict & Win entry also scores season points automatically when you
enter the final score (End Match): **3 points** for the exact score, **1 point**
for the correct result (home win, draw or away win). There is nothing extra to
do. The table is at https://lobistarsfc.com/predict-and-win/league and each
fan sees their own position on their account page.

- Ties: more exact scores first, then whoever predicted earliest on average.
- Disqualified entries and accounts marked **club staff/player** are left out
  of the table, just as they can't win the match prize.
- Correcting a final score or disqualifying an entry updates the table straight away.
- **End-of-season prize:** not set yet. When the club confirms one, enter it in
  the CMS: **Site Settings & Shop → Prediction League prize**
  (amount in naira; 0 hides it). It then appears on the league page and in the
  Predict & Win terms.

## 7. If something goes wrong

- **Fans say they can't vote:** voting only works between Start Match and
  End Match, for fans who are logged in with a confirmed email. Each fan has
  one vote per match.
- **A player is missing from voting:** add them to the matchday squad and press Save.
- **The stream doesn't play:** check the link in Match Centre. Some Facebook
  videos can't be shown on other websites; fans can use the "Open on Facebook" link.
- **The match is stuck on LIVE:** press End Match. It also closes itself 2h15 after kick-off.
- **Reminder emails didn't go out:** the email service (Resend) must be set up
  by the website manager. Admin → Predict & Win shows whether each reminder was sent.
- **You were signed out of the admin:** it happens after 30 minutes without
  activity and after 12 hours. Log in again; nothing you saved is lost. Sign in
  again shortly before kick-off so a long match day doesn't run past 12 hours.
  The Kitchen Screen and the Live Order Board stay signed in while they're open.
- **"Account locked":** 5 wrong passwords or codes lock an account for 15
  minutes. Wait, or ask a Super Admin to press **Unlock** (Settings › Staff
  Users & Roles). Lost your phone for two-step login? Use one of your backup codes.
